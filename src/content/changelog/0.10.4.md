## 0.10.4 — 2026-09-10

A round of fixes from watching alerts and the home screen against a real estate. Most of them come down to the same thing: what a busy channel or a busy namespace actually looks like.

### Alerts

- **Chat alerts read in one line.** Slack and Discord alerts were a grid of fields, five lines each, and a channel scrolls past twenty at a time. Each one now leads with the count and the queue — *724,455 messages on order-handling-commands / orderprocessor* — then the namespace, set and rule in a small grey line, then links to the Azure portal and to viewbus.
- **Queues that are full on purpose stop raising backlog alerts.** Some subscriptions are parking lots: a wiretap nobody consumes, or a test subscription left enrolled, sitting at tens of thousands of messages forever. An ordinary wobble on a backlog like that looked like an anomaly to the smart backlog alert. A queue now has to have drained at least once in the last 24 hours before that alert can fire. A queue filling up from empty still fires straight away.
- **A backlog alert names its count correctly.** It no longer describes itself as dead-letter activity.

### Monitored sets

- **Exempt the queues you don't want watched.** A namespace usually has one or two entities that are backed up on purpose. Instead of listing the other forty by hand, watch the namespace and exempt those two. An exemption always wins, whatever order you add things in.
- **Pick targets by searching.** The set editor is one search box over everything you can monitor, with **Watch** and **Exempt** on every result. Clicking the other one on something already picked switches it over.

### Home screen

- **The Live card can rank by active backlog.** It only ever showed dead letters. It now has the same **Dead letters / Active** tabs as the dashboard, and remembers your choice. In Active, each queue is coloured by what's normal for that queue — *"1,204 active — normally ~15"* — exactly as on the dashboard.

All local-first as always. Update via the in-app updater or grab a fresh installer from [viewbus.app](https://viewbus.app/) — your settings, themes, monitored sets, and stored connections carry over untouched.
