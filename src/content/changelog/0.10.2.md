## 0.10.2 — 2026-09-05

One fix, to the monitoring dashboard's list.

### Fixes

- **No more column of zeros.** The list sorted by one number and hid rows by another. The "carrying DL" chip kept every queue holding dead letters, while the default sort, *Most new* over the last hour, ranks by how many arrived in that hour. On a real estate those barely overlap. So the dashboard opened on row after row of `0`, sorted by a number none of them had, with their real totals in small red text underneath. The chip now hides by whatever you're sorting on. Under *Most new* it keeps the queues with new dead letters, and its label says so: **197 with new DL**. The empty-state message names the rule that hid the rows, too.
- **A zero is never red.** The big number's colour came from the queue's total, so a queue holding 258,715 dead letters with none new this hour showed a bright red `0`. The big number is now coloured by its own size. The total's warning colour stays on the line underneath, where the total is shown.
