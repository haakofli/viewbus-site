## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-27

### A refreshed look

- **Floating panels and sidebar sections** replace the sidebar tabs. Settings is now a sidebar mode, not a dialog.
- **Search-first home.** Search from the home screen, with your recent searches and opened resources. Spotlight uses the same search panel.
- **A clearer namespace view:** totals at the top, then a table grouped by topic.
- **A tidier message view.** Body, properties and system share one tab bar, actions on ticked messages sit in a bar over the list, and the detail panel docks to the bottom when the window is narrow.

### viewbus and your AI

- **One-click connect.** viewbus finds 12 AI clients, including Claude, Codex and Cursor, and can connect, repair or remove itself from Settings. It offers once on launch.
- **"Show me."** An assistant can open viewbus on the queue or messages it's talking about.
- **Reads across the whole tenant.** The MCP can search any subscription, not just the selected one, and answer "where are the dead letters?" in one call.
- **AI alert review (opt-in per monitored set).** Your own Claude or Codex CLI reads an alert first and writes the message you get. If the CLI is missing or fails, the alert is sent as normal.

### Monitoring is now Insights

- **Every subscription is sampled by default**, so Insights works without setup. You can still pause any source.
- **The resource Monitoring and Operations tabs** say whether things look OK, what can run, and what ran.

### Fixes

- **The first Ctrl+I no longer freezes viewbus** on Windows.
- **Resending or deleting picked dead letters is reliable**, however many there are and however deep they sit, and one broker blip no longer kills a large resend.

All local-first as always. Update via the in-app updater or grab a fresh installer from [viewbus.app](https://viewbus.app/). Your settings, themes, monitored sets and stored connections carry over untouched.
