## 0.5.1 — 2026-05-06

### Fixes

- The diff editor used by **Resend** and **Compose** rendered as a
  blank pane in release builds — a CSP rule was blocking Monaco's
  bundled worker and fonts. Dev builds were unaffected, which is why
  it slipped past local testing.
