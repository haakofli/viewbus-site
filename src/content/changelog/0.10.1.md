## 0.10.1 — 2026-09-04

Every alert type so far asked for a number: "more than 50 dead letters", "more than 10 new in an hour". You can't pick that number well until you've watched the queue for a week. And a wrong number is how an alert becomes noise people learn to ignore. Smart Alerts asks for nothing and learns the queue instead.

### Smart Alerts

- **It fires when a queue starts behaving unlike itself.** It watches how fast dead letters *arrive*, not how many are sitting there. A queue that always gains a million a day never fires. One that normally gains five and suddenly gains five hundred does.
- **It has to be surprising and big enough.** A single dead letter on a silent queue is statistically remarkable and practically meaningless. So an alert needs both an unusual arrival rate for that queue and a minimum number of dead letters. Choose **Strict**, **Balanced** (the default) or **Sensitive** to set how unusual is unusual.
- **It's the default for a new alert.** The other types are still there for when you do know the number. Your existing alerts aren't touched.
- **It says when it's still learning.** A new smart alert needs about five hours of history before it can judge anything. Until then the Rules pane shows **"Learning — N more samples"** instead of looking armed.
- **Every alert shows the baseline it was judged against**, so you can see why it fired.

### While viewbus was closed

- **Dead letters that piled up overnight aren't treated as a spike.** viewbus can't know when they arrived, so a gap never fires an alert.
- **You get one summary in its place.** When viewbus starts again, it compares what arrived during the gap with what's normal for a gap that long. The queues that gained far more than usual are listed in one entry in the notification bell, most unusual first. A queue that gained its usual overnight amount isn't listed.

All local-first as always. Update via the in-app updater or grab a fresh installer from [viewbus.app](https://viewbus.app/) — your settings, themes, monitored sets, and stored connections carry over untouched.
